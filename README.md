# Privacy Policy for UFO Ride to Infinite Space

**Effective Date:** November 25, 2026

 **UFO Ride to Infinite Space** as an arcade space game. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
* **Microphone & Audio Data :** When you activate the in-game **NOVA Voice Co-Pilot**, the app accesses your device's microphone to stream real-time voice audio for interactive AI co-pilot responses. Audio is processed transiently to generate responses and is not permanently stored on our servers.
* **Gameplay & Cloud Save Data:** We store game progress, high scores, unlocked UFO skins, upgrades, daily rewards, and achievements locally on your device and via cloud save services so you can sync progress across sessions.
* **Multiplayer & Diagnostics Data:** For multiplayer duels and app stability, we may collect temporary session identifiers, device type, OS version, and crash logs.

## 2. Third-Party Services & AI Processing
Our game integrates third-party APIs to power live features. These providers process data strictly to deliver the requested functionality:
* **Google Gemini API (Voice Co-Pilot & Lyria Music Studio):** Voice inputs and text prompts submitted to the NOVA Voice Co-Pilot or Lyria Music Studio are transmitted securely to Google's AI services to generate real-time speech and custom soundtracks. View the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Google Play Services:** Used for app distribution, updates, and basic crash reporting.

## 3. Permissions We Request
* **Microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):** Optional permission requested only when you tap the Co-Pilot or HUD mic button. You can revoke this permission anytime in your Android device settings.
* **Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`):** Required for multiplayer duels, cloud saves, AI voice chat, and music generation.

## 4. Children's Privacy
UFO Ride to Infinite Space is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information or voice recordings from children.

## 5. Data Security & Retention
All network communications between the app and backend servers use HTTPS/WSS encryption. Voice audio is processed in real time and discarded immediately after generating the AI response. Cloud save data is retained until you request deletion or uninstall/clear app data.
